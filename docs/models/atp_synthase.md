`easymode segment atp_synthase`

The ATP synthase model segments ATP synthase complexes in the inner mitochondrial membrane. It has not yet been validated by subtomogram averaging. It may be worth combining the output of this model with that of the [mitochondrion model](mitochondrion.md). In case the ATP synthase model outputs spurious blobs outside of mitochondria, you then know which blobs to ignore.
